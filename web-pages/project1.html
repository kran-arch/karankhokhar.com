Portfolio Website

Built entirely by hand using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—without templates or page builders—this portfolio was crafted to master core front-end fundamentals. Focusing on clean semantic structure, responsive layouts, and a distinct visual identity, it served as a hands-on project to gain fluency in web development while doubling as an interactive resume.

HTML CSS JavaScript

What it does

A multi-paged site with sections for About, Education, Skills, and Projects, plus a blueprint-inspired design with a title block, and animated skill gauges.

Inspiration

The design and core inspiration for this portfolio draw heavily from internet personality and engineer Gazi jarin.Known for her popular developer content, Gazi is a New York-based Software Engineer at Google and a creative technologist who builds projects across machine learning, hardware, and game development.

Her portfolio stands out in the developer community for its sleek, dark-mode visual identity, clean typography, minimalist layout, and thoughtful presentation of personal projects, art, and professional milestones. Modeling my portfolio after her work provided both a high standard for aesthetic design and a blueprint for showcasing technical expertise alongside personal identity.
